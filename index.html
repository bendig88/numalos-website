About PT. Numalos Abadi

A specialised engineering company serving Indonesia's palm oil industry from Medan since 1990.

OUR STORY

Built around engineering and excellent after-sales service

PT. Numalos Abadi is a specialised engineering company that represents a number of prominent brands in Indonesia. One of them is Flottweg SE of Germany, a leader and pioneer in decanter technology for palm oil mills in Indonesia.

Established in 1990, the company was formed to provide specialised engineering and excellent after-sales service for the brands it represents. To meet growing demand and serve customers better, we opened a workshop with state-of-the-art machines at our headquarters in Kawasan Industri Medan (KIM), North Sumatra, in 1994.

MILESTONES

Decades of growth in North Sumatra

1981 Flottweg supplies its first machines to a customer in North Sumatra. They are still running today.
1990 PT. Numalos Abadi is established in Medan.
1994 Our workshop opens at the KIM headquarters.
2006 Partnership with FLSmidth Krebs begins, bringing desanding cyclones to major palm oil companies.
Today More than 500 Flottweg machines sold in Indonesia together with Flottweg.

Our products

Separation and processing equipment from world-class principals, supplied and serviced locally.

Flottweg decanter centrifuges

Flottweg delivered its first decanter in 1956 and has since become one of the world's leading decanter manufacturers. Its headquarters is in Vilsbiburg, near Munich. Unlike many competitors, Flottweg still designs, manufactures and assembles its machines in Germany, which is why they are known for reliability and durability.

Patented Variable Impeller System: adjust the liquid-liquid separation line while running at full speed In Indonesia since 1981, with the first machines still in operation More than 500 units sold in Indonesia by Flottweg and PT. Numalos Abadi
Request a quote Visit flottweg.com

FLSmidth Krebs desanding cyclones

With more than 55 years in the industry, Krebs is a world leader in cyclones and centrifugal slurry pumps, respected for innovative engineering and quality products. In 2007 Krebs joined the FLSmidth group and is now known as FLSmidth Krebs.

We have worked with FLSmidth Krebs since 2006, supplying desanding cyclones to many major palm oil companies in Indonesia. First used in mining, Krebs gMAX desanding cyclones are known for separation efficiency, robustness, endurance and reliability.

Our divisions

Four departments working together so your machines keep running.

01

Sales Department

The Sales department's main task is to promote new machines to our customers. Beyond technical knowledge of the machines, our sales team understands the applications they will be used in, so every recommendation fits the process.

02

Technical Service Department

Our technicians and electricians commission and start up new machines, and provide after-sales service including on-site repairs and spare part installation. They are trained in-house, then continue with hands-on training at mills to learn the process flow of each application.

Customer service has always been our main focus, so every service engineer has a utility vehicle and a driver for greater mobility and faster response. The team keeps growing as more machines are sold into the market.

03

Workshop Department

Officially opened in 1994, our workshop was built to strengthen after-sales service for the machines we sell, especially Flottweg decanters. It lets us recondition machine components in-house, including decanter scrolls, efficiently and on time.

We keep improving it by adding more modern and efficient machines, tightening quality control and training our workshop staff.

04

Warehouse Department

A delay in servicing because parts are unavailable means longer downtime and real economic losses for our customers. Our warehouse keeps an adequate, sustainable stock of spare parts ready, in line with our commitment to excellent after-sales service.
